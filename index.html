🛒 How to buy smart

  • Look for a third-party seal. Supplements aren't tested by the FDA before sale. NSF Certified for Sport, USP Verified and Informed Sport mean an outside lab checked the product.
  • Check a product yourself: NSF Certified for Sport ↗ · Informed Sport search ↗
  • Cheapest places for basics (vitamin D, magnesium, fish oil, creatine): store brands at Costco (Kirkland), Target (up&up), Walmart (Equate) and Amazon.
  • Pharmacies (CVS, Walgreens) are good for vitamins, fiber and melatonin you need today. GNC and iHerb carry more sports and herbal items.
  • Endurance fuel (gels, electrolytes): REI or your local run/bike shop let you try flavors before race day.
  • Skip "proprietary blends" that hide doses, and anything promising rapid fat loss or hormone boosts.

Stack is for general information, not medical advice. Supplements aren't FDA-approved for safety or effectiveness before sale. Check with a doctor or pharmacist before starting anything, especially if you're pregnant, under 18, or take medication.
